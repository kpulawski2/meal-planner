# Updating a meal plan

Settings now has direct selectors for each active meal. Choose a named meal to
repeat, tuna wraps and salads for lunch variety, or chicken dinner variety.
**Update my plan** uses the saved budget, period, meal count, household and
nutrition settings. Changing a budget or preset refreshes the summary at once;
settings and meal choices persist across reloads. Existing meals remain visible
until a replacement passes the checks, with an explicit update message.

Switching from three meals to two uses Lunch and Dinner. Switching back restores
a Breakfast slot. Kept meals, quantities and cooking records follow their meal
roles. A cooked meal or day cannot disappear: the app explains that a new week
is needed before removing it. An inactive uncooked meal does not leave a lock on
the wrong slot. Undoing cooking after a supported move still restores stock.

A failed search keeps the settings the user explicitly chose in Settings or the
manual meal editor. The older tailored editor opens with current settings, not
an old saved brief's meal count. Chat drafts rebase onto settings changed since
that conversation. Chat proposals remain private until a complete valid plan is
accepted. Late responses are discarded when the underlying settings, recipes,
stock, locks or plan change. Background refreshes preserve an active input.

The exact FUEL10K chocolate flakes and milk breakfast is available in the meal
selector. A saved £2.50/350 g ASDA cereal pack can be included as an explicitly
separate ASDA purchase when planning at Lidl or Aldi. This is not a Lidl/Aldi
product, price or stock claim. Tuna Crunch Wrap and Tuna Crunch Salad have
measured recipes. The sourced Aldi four-pack costs £2.49 for **408 g drained**
contents; its 580 g net label is not treated as edible tuna. Original source
dates and the existing Aldi-for-Lidl estimate labels stay visible.

A checked seven-day example repeats the cereal breakfast, tuna crunch salad and
frozen chicken teriyaki dinner. It meets the 1,600 kcal ceiling and 150 g protein
minimum at approximately **£33.97 in full packs**, within £35. Portions are
adjusted and visible per meal. A varied version can cost more because it needs
more different packs. If a checked basket exceeds the budget, the app reports
that total and keeps the edited settings; it never silently raises the budget,
reduces protein or changes an explicit meal count. These are saved reference
prices and a bounded search, not guaranteed shelf prices or a global minimum.
