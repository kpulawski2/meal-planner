# Saving chosen meals

Select meals in Settings → Daily meal choices, set portions per person with
Meal portions, then choose Review chosen meals → Save these meals. Review is
also available from Today and Plan when every meal type has a selection.
The review includes the period, exact portions, daily nutrition and any target
differences. It preserves kept/cooked meals and existing cooking records,
pantry stock, purchases and diary entries. The shopping checklist and prep tasks
refresh for the new plan. An incomplete selection needs a meal for each empty
slot, or the automatic planner can find those meals.

Explicit meal choices can be saved even if a nutrition goal is missed or a
shopping price is missing. These plans are marked as chosen meals. They do not
claim to have passed an automatic nutrition/budget check. Shopping quantities are
available immediately; the existing catalogue checks prices separately and
keeps missing prices visible. The saved budget is unchanged.

Automatic planning remains strict. Find a plan to match targets checks nutrition,
diet, equipment, time, portions, variety and the full-pack budget before saving.
Failures now show the actual cause, including compatible-price gaps and the
selected meals' nutrition differences. In assistant chat, a failed automatic
check offers Review chosen meals for a complete set of selections; saving still
requires the explicit review action. Stale reviews and late requests cannot
replace newer settings or stock. A storage failure leaves the prior plan intact.

## Calorie goals and the reported example

Settings shows a choice between a calorie ceiling and a ±10% range. New profiles
and older profiles with no explicit mode use a ceiling. A saved explicit range
stays selected until the user changes it. Protein is a minimum; maximum fat is
visible directly in Settings. Changing the calorie number updates the mode labels.

At the shipped recipe quantities, two FUEL10K Chocolate Flakes & Milk portions,
one Tuna Protein Pasta Salad and one Chicken Enchilada Rice Bake total about
1,761 kcal, 164.8 g protein and 26.1 g fat daily. Those satisfy a 2,000 kcal ceiling,
150 g protein minimum and 40 g fat maximum. The 2,000 kcal range has a 1,800 kcal
minimum: the review explains the 39 kcal difference without blocking a chosen
plan. These are ingredient estimates, not individually measured nutrition.

Cereal amounts describe one portion. With 70 g cereal and 200 g weighed skimmed
milk per portion, a two-portion breakfast uses 140 g cereal and 400 g milk;
the full example day is about 1,911 kcal. The cereal editor shows this total and
retains the saved breakfast portion count. Change breakfast to one portion if
the entered quantities describe the entire bowl you want to eat.

No additional credential, paid service, database, native app rebuild or signing
change is required. Offline cache v57 includes the shared manual-plan module.
