# Recording purchased food

In Shopping list, tick what you bought and choose **Shopping done · add to
pantry**. Review the actual ingredient, product, number of packs, usable amount
per pack and storage. Use drained weights for drained tuna. This review works
when matching is offline, loading or unavailable, and with reference prices.

The amount paid is for the whole line, including all its packs. Catalogue
prices appear only as suggestions. Leave a paid amount blank to record stock
without claiming a known expense. The budget and purchase history retain that
unknown amount rather than showing zero. Confirmed purchases support Undo and
survive reload. Storage failures and stale reviews do not partially add food.

For Lidl, the review can save confirmed paid pack prices into the existing
device-local notebook. Its planning opt-in stays as selected. Standard pack
prices are saved only where the paid amount divides into whole pennies per
pack. These observations retain their date and receipt source and do not
become official website listings.

The price notebook includes sanitised observations from the 17 October 2026
receipt. Payment information and the receipt image are not stored in the app.
The shopper confirmed 450 g skyr (£1.49), 1 kg frozen chicken (£4.25), 100 g
drained tuna per tin (£0.59; 24 tins), and two six-packs of free-range mixed
eggs (£0.99 each). Purchase review has a button to fill these actual quantities,
plus the loose weights and cucumber count visible on that receipt. Frozen
chicken stays separate from fresh chicken; lighter products stay separate from
full-fat recipe ingredients. Other pack sizes still need confirmation.

Sesame oil and rice vinegar each cost £1.15 after their £0.84 reductions. The
transcribed line amounts total £48.23 against a printed total of £48.33. The
remaining 10p is explicitly unresolved and is not assigned to a product.
