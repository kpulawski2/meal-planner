# Expanded methods and TikTok-style collection

The curated library now contains 521 recipes. The detailed-method overlay covers 426 existing recipes: the original 100 plus the remaining 326 meals and snacks. Ten fruit entries, the simple cereal breakfast and 24 already detailed CREAMi recipes stay concise or retain their specialist instructions. Public web imports and custom recipes keep their source/user instructions.

The expanded methods retain their existing ingredient lists and yields. They clarify grain weights, drained pulses, cooking heat, listed seasonings, wrapping, doneness and storage. The ingredient/yield guard remains in place, as do source credits and licences. Ready-cooked `Lentils` are distinguished from dry `Red lentils`. Unlisted optional oil, milk, salt and spice additions were removed from several older methods.

Five older recipe names now match their ingredients: Turkey & Pepper Rice Bake (frozen pepper pieces), Warm Edamame Snack (no listed salt), Ham & Cucumber Roll-Ups, Chicken Rice & Crunch Salad Bowl (no listed shawarma spice) and Beef Cottage Pie Bowl. Their old names remain search tags. Ingredients, IDs, yields and cooked-meal signatures are preserved. A generic edible-orange nutrition estimate fills a previously missing profile; it is labelled as an estimate.

`public/viral-recipes.js` adds 60 original measured social-food formulations:

- 26 dinners, including three air-fryer and three slow-cooker meals.
- 14 lunches, including eight four-wrap freezer batches.
- 10 breakfasts, including overnight oats, baked oats, pancakes and savoury eggs.
- 10 snacks/desserts, including yoghurt bark, frozen clusters and mousse-style pots.

They join the existing TikTok ideas collection and have a **New TikTok-style** filter in Filters & sort. These are independent recipes inspired by social-food formats, not copied creator recipes or verified video nutrition claims. All used fats, sauces, coatings and toppings are measured, nutrition is calculated from ingredients, and timings remain estimates rather than kitchen-tested claims.

New recipes use the same equipment, preference, dislike, variety, fixed-batch and budget checks as the rest of the library. Browser tests cover discovery, batch quantities, source credits, cooking mode, saved-data preservation and offline use. App shell v53 precaches both the expanded methods and the new collection.

The bounded planner sample now preserves two protein-dense choices from each meal/source/protein group when macro targets are active. This prevents a growing collection from randomly displacing the affordable lean dinner needed alongside a preferred burrito lunch. The existing real-food regression confirms seven burrito lunches plus cereal within 1,600 kcal, at least 150 g protein daily and a £45 whole-pack budget. Hard constraints and the 250-recipe limit remain unchanged.

Aggregate multi-request stress-test deadlines allow the slower managed execution host to finish; per-search timing, health responsiveness and memory assertions remain in place. The unmodified main branch reproduced the former aggregate timeouts on the same host.
