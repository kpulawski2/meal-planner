# CREAMi collection

The 24 recipes in `public/creami-recipes.js` are original Meal Planner formulations. Their text is available under CC0-1.0. They are not copied Ninja recipes and have not been physically tested in a kitchen.

Each measured ingredient list makes **one standard-size tub, divided into two servings**. Nutrition is calculated per serving from ingredient estimates; the whole-tub figure is twice that value. Whole-week shopping rounds the total planned servings for each CREAMi recipe up to complete two-serving tubs. Any unused portion is a frozen leftover, rather than an unpriced half-tub. Nut butter, chocolate, biscuits, honey and the 30 ml finishing liquid are included. These desserts do not all have the same energy content and are not automatically labelled low calorie.

Active preparation takes 10–15 minutes. Every base also needs at least 24 hours of freezing. The CREAMi equipment setting must be enabled before the planner can select these recipes. The app can still show the collection for browsing without selecting it for a plan.

## Appliance technique references

The [NC300UK safety guide](https://support.ninjakitchen.co.uk/hc/en-gb/article_attachments/5111589338524/NC300UK_IB_Sheet.pdf) and [Deluxe guide](https://support.ninjakitchen.co.uk/hc/en-gb/article_attachments/12401152639004) require model-compatible containers, level freezing and the model's fill limits. Hard loose frozen ingredients and plain blocks of ice are unsuitable. Fruit in this collection is blended into a continuous base before freezing.

Ninja's [protein ice cream overview](https://www.sharkninja.co.uk/blog/protein-ice-cream.html), [frozen yoghurt example](https://www.sharkninja.co.uk/vanilla-frozen-yogurt/REC10600EU.html) and [fruit sorbet example](https://www.sharkninja.co.uk/no-prep-mango-sorbet/REC12062EU.html) informed the collection's techniques. They are reference links, not claims that Ninja endorses these formulations. Use scoop mode; no Swirl dispensing compatibility is claimed.

The batch fits within the original model's nominal 493 ml tub capacity. Actual blended volume varies; the physical MAX FILL mark takes precedence. A Deluxe requires its own container and appropriate processing zone; do not double this collection into an original-size tub. Recipe methods count the reserved finishing milk as a required addition, so no extra liquid or topping is silently omitted from the calculation.
