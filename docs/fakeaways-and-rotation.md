# Fakeaways, burrito batches and fresh weeks

Recipes → TikTok ideas → Recipe style now includes **Fakeaways** and
**Meal-prep burritos**. Thirty measured recipes cover crispy chilli chicken,
shawarma fries, peri-peri rice, curry, Cajun pasta, burger wraps, sweet-and-sour
chicken, satay noodles, pizza wraps, beef bowls, salmon and tofu.

Six four-portion burrito batches cover buffalo chicken, smoky honey chicken,
beef and bean, chicken tikka, BBQ chicken and egg/potato breakfast burritos.
Shopping rounds these recipes to complete batches, including every wrap, oil,
cheese and sauce. Eating one burrito records one portion's nutrition; cooking
one batch buys four and supports storing the extra portions. Methods include
cooling, freezing, defrosting and reheating guidance, with stricter rice storage.

These are original measured Meal Planner formulations, with calculated ingredient
nutrition and estimated timings, not kitchen-tested recipes or copied creator
macro claims. Six ideas credit Jalal Sam Fit's public collection:
https://jalalsamfit.jupiter.shop/
The credit identifies the inspiration and explicitly distinguishes our quantities
and method from the creator's exact recipe. Other recipes use original fakeaway
formulations. Public TikTok captions were discoverable, but individual video pages
were inaccessible during research; no unverified TikTok video identities are used.

**Prefer fresh recipes each week** is enabled by default in Meals for this plan.
When you finish a plan, choose **Start new week**. The last three archived plans
provide bounded recipe/flavour recency scores. These guide both the 250-recipe
selection and the budget worker; complete histories stay on the device. Chicken
is not penalised as one flavour: shawarma, peri-peri, Cajun, curry and barbecue can
rotate independently. Two-week plans also prefer dishes/flavours not used in
week one. Existing saved plans do not change merely by opening the updated app.

Specific daily repeats, scheduled meals and locks still apply. Automatic tuna
wrap/salad and chicken-dinner groups refresh their matching options so saved
selection lists cannot exclude newly added meals forever. Freshness remains a
preference: budget, diet, equipment, portions and nutrition are hard limits, and
recent meals can be used when needed. Turn the preference off to stop using
recent plans for rotation. Existing diary and extra-meal actions are unchanged.
