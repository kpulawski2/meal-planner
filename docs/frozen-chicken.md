# Frozen chicken meals

The meal finder and weekly planner include five measured frozen chicken dinners:
teriyaki rice, tomato curry and rice, tomato pasta, BBQ potato bowl and peanut
noodles. Search **frozen chicken** in Recipes, or use **Try frozen chicken
dinners** in the one-off meal finder. They use whole, plain, unbreaded fillets
and cook them separately in the oven before combining with hot sides. No oil or
garnishes are assumed. Preparation is 15 minutes plus about 45 minutes oven time;
the exact pack instructions take priority. An oven and hob are required.

have different added-water content and nutrition. Prices do not confirm stock

retain their original dates.

In an Aldi basket these are saved Aldi references. In a Lidl basket the frozen
bag is explicitly an **Aldi estimate for Lidl**, used only when no compatible
Lidl price exists. It does not establish a Lidl product, price or stock. Fresh
recipes continue to use fresh packs. Whole-pack costs, leftovers and shared
bags are calculated by the same purchasing code as other ingredients. A cheap
per-kg bag does not always give the cheapest checkout for a small meal.

The one-person frozen teriyaki dinner uses 250 g frozen chicken, 55 g dry rice,
180 g frozen mixed vegetables and 20 g teriyaki sauce. With current saved Lidl
references plus labelled Aldi estimates, full packs cost **£7.15**: chicken
£4.25, rice £0.52, vegetables £0.99 and sauce £1.39. Its fresh counterpart's
full-pack basket is £9.34. These are reference-price examples, not live shelf
guarantees. Unpriced ingredients remain excluded from affordable results.

Frozen nutrition uses the [ASDA 1 kg frozen fillet pack's serving declaration](https://www.asda.com/groceries/product/frozen-breasts-portions-thighs-joints/asda-chicken-breast-fillets-1kg/2725330):
an ovenbaked 1/8 pack supplies 115 kcal, 21 g protein, 0.8 g carbohydrate and
2.9 g fat. That portion corresponds to 125 g of the frozen pack; per 100 g
**frozen/as-purchased** the estimate is 92 kcal, 16.8 g protein, 0.64 g carbs and
2.32 g fat. The cooked-per-100-g table is not applied to frozen weights.
The teriyaki dinner estimates 563 kcal and 52.3 g protein. The recipe and UI
state that other packs, including Aldi's added-water fillets, can differ.
This does not make exact macro guarantees for substituted products.

Pack preparation notes appear in weekly shopping cards, copies, CSV exports,
one-off results and the planner's warnings. Frozen shelf metadata places a
confirmed purchase in the freezer even when its name omits “frozen”. Reference
estimates still cannot record an actual purchase or spending. Cooked strips,
breaded and flavoured products are excluded from the plain frozen breast match.

The recipes serve immediately and retain any pack prohibition on reheating.
They do not instruct users to put raw frozen poultry in a slow cooker. The
[Food Standards Agency](https://www.food.gov.uk/safety-hygiene/cooking-your-food)
lists 75°C for 30 seconds as an appropriate core cooking temperature/time.
