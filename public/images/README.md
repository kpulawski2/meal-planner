# Recipe serving illustrations

`serving-ideas.png` is an original AI-generated serving-illustration atlas created
for this application on 8 November 2026. There are three columns and two rows,
with square 512-pixel tiles in a 1536 × 1024 image: overnight oats, chocolate
cereal with milk, tuna pasta salad, chicken enchilada bake, chicken burrito,
and chicken curry. The SVG viewport preserves each tile's aspect ratio.

The app labels these as serving illustrations and identifies AI generation in
recipe details. They do not specify actual ingredients, portions or nutrition.
Users' own locally stored food photos take priority.
